📖 The story

Once upon a time, three bears lived in a little house in the woods. There was Daddy Bear, Mummy Bear and Baby Bear.

One morning, Mummy Bear made porridge for breakfast. The porridge was too hot to eat, so the three bears went for a walk while it cooled.

A little girl called Goldilocks was walking in the woods. She saw the little house. The door was open, so she went inside.

On the table were three bowls of porridge. Goldilocks tasted Daddy Bear’s porridge from the big bowl. It was too hot. She tasted Mummy Bear’s porridge from the middle bowl. It was too cold. Then she tasted Baby Bear’s porridge from the small bowl. It was just right, so she ate it all up.

Next she saw three chairs. Daddy Bear’s chair was too hard. Mummy Bear’s chair was too soft. Baby Bear’s chair was just right — but when she sat down, it broke!

Goldilocks felt sleepy. She went upstairs to the bedroom and saw three beds. Daddy Bear’s bed was too hard. Mummy Bear’s bed was too soft. Baby Bear’s bed was just right, and Goldilocks fell fast asleep.

Soon the three bears came home. “Someone has eaten my porridge!” cried Baby Bear. “Someone has broken my chair! And someone is sleeping in my bed!”

Goldilocks woke up and saw the three bears. She was scared. She jumped out of bed, ran down the stairs and out of the door, and she ran all the way home.
